The Challenge & Vision

Why Circl Was Conceived

Modern suburban and urban communities suffer from an unintended side effect of development: hyper-isolation and massive material waste.

We purchase expensive products that sit idle 99% of the time, and we lack a secure, structured way to interact, exchange value, or ask for simple favours from the people living just 50 meters away.

Material Underutilization

An average domestic power drill is used for only 13 minutes in its entire lifespan. Circl unlocks these dormant community inventories.

The "Stranger Danger" Friction

People want to help and share, but fear damage, theft, or scams. Circl builds a dedicated trust engine to facilitate hyper-local sharing safely.

The Product Ecosystem

A Dual-Pillared Marketplace

Physical Resource Sharing
Borrowing (Goodwill):

Free, neighborly lending of basic household items, books, and appliances to build local rapport.

Renting (Monetized):

Secure hire of high-value tools, vehicles, or gear with system-managed financial deposit protections.

Collaborative Local Services
Friendly Favours:

Volunteer aid (pet-feeding, plant-watering, elderly check-ins) to support the neighborhood network.

Micro-tasks & Gigs:

Paid assignments (yard cleaning, technical setups, local delivery) creating gig economy jobs right at home.

Under the Hood

The Technological Toolset

Circl is built on a highly modern, reactive stack optimized for lightning-fast mobile performance, geolocation, and security.

Flutter

Cross-platform client engine delivering smooth, high-fidelity UI animations on iOS and Android.

Supabase

PostgreSQL database, real-time chat sync, file storage, and secure authentication backend.

Google Maps

Geospatial matching, live distance calculations, and interactive map interfaces.

PayHere SDK

Mobile SDK handling secure checkout, credit cards, and escrow safety holds.

ML Kit OCR

Extracts text and validates authenticity of national identity cards (NICs) instantly.

The Trust Framework

Aura: The Community Reputation Engine

To eliminate friction, Circl features a complete validation, safety escrow, and gamification framework.

01

NIC Verification

Google ML Kit OCR processes government-issued cards, ensuring every profile maps to a verified real-world individual.

02

Secure Escrow Holds

High-value tool rentals hold a pre-authorized security deposit. Money is released only after successful return and inspection.

03

Aura Point Economy

Users earn points and level up their "Aura" by completing tasks, sharing items, and accumulating high ratings. High Aura unlocks reduced deposit requirements and premium badges.

Looking Forward
